# Topiltzin Flores - Muscle Gain Program

**Goal:** Muscle Gain  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Duration:** 60 minutes (incl. warm-up & cool-down)  
**Frequency:** 6 days/week  
**Limitations:** Elbow pain — avoid heavy isolation exercises for forearms/triceps  

---

## Weekly Split: Upper/Lower (3x Upper, 3x Lower)

### Warm-up (5 min - every session)
- 2 min light cardio (treadmill/bike)
- 3 min dynamic stretching (arm circles, leg swings, cat-cow)

---

## UPPER BODY DAYS (Monday, Wednesday, Friday)

### Monday - Chest & Back Focus
1. **Barbell Bench Press** - 4 × 6-8 reps - Rest 2 min
   - Form tip: Full range of motion, chest to bar, feet planted
   
2. **Bent-Over Barbell Rows** - 4 × 6-8 reps - Rest 2 min
   - Form tip: Hinge at hips, squeeze shoulder blades, keep core tight
   
3. **Dumbbell Incline Press** - 3 × 8-10 reps - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Control descent, elbows at 45° angle
   
4. **Lat Pulldown (Machine)** - 3 × 8-10 reps - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Pull to chest, avoid swinging, full stretch at top
   
5. **Cable Chest Fly** - 3 × 10-12 reps - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: Slight elbow bend, arc motion, squeeze at center

---

### Wednesday - Back & Shoulders
1. **Deadlifts (Conventional)** - 4 × 5-6 reps - Rest 2 min
   - Form tip: Neutral spine, shoulders over bar, drive through heels
   
2. **Machine Shoulder Press** - 4 × 8-10 reps - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Controlled tempo, no arching, full range
   
3. **Face Pulls** - 3 × 12-15 reps - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: External rotation, rear delts squeeze, light weight (elbow-friendly)
   
4. **Vertical Chest Machine** - 3 × 8-10 reps - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Controlled movement, mid-chest engagement
   
5. **Rear Delt Fly Machine** - 3 × 10-12 reps - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: Smooth motion, mind-muscle connection

---

### Friday - Upper Body Power
1. **Incline Barbell Press** - 4 × 6-8 reps - Rest 2 min
   - Form tip: 30° angle, controlled descent, explosive press
   
2. **Weighted Pull-ups** - 4 × 6-8 reps - Rest 2 min
   - Form tip: Full range, chest to bar, avoid momentum
   
3. **Dumbbell Rows** - 3 × 8-10 reps (each side) - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Elbow path close to body, squeeze at top
   
4. **Machine Lateral Raise** - 3 × 10-12 reps - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: Smooth, no jerking, elbows slightly bent (avoid elbow stress)
   
5. **Chest Dips (assisted if needed)** - 3 × 8-10 reps - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Controlled, lean slightly forward, full range

---

## LOWER BODY DAYS (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday)

### Tuesday - Legs Compound
1. **Barbell Back Squat** - 4 × 6-8 reps - Rest 2 min
   - Form tip: Knees track over toes, chest up, ATG depth
   
2. **Romanian Deadlifts** - 4 × 8-10 reps - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Hinge movement, slight knee bend, hamstring stretch
   
3. **Leg Press** - 3 × 8-10 reps - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Full range, knees in line, controlled descent
   
4. **Leg Curls (Machine)** - 3 × 10-12 reps - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: Squeeze hamstrings, smooth motion, no swinging
   
5. **Calf Raises** - 3 × 12-15 reps - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: Full stretch at bottom, explosive push-off

---

### Thursday - Leg Power
1. **Barbell Front Squat** - 4 × 6-8 reps - Rest 2 min
   - Form tip: Upright torso, elbows high, deep squat
   
2. **Leg Curl (Lying)** - 4 × 8-10 reps - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Full range, controlled descent, squeeze
   
3. **Walking Lunges (Dumbbells)** - 3 × 10 reps per leg - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Step length, back knee hover, upright torso
   
4. **Leg Extension** - 3 × 10-12 reps - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: Full extension, controlled tempo
   
5. **Seated Calf Raises** - 3 × 15-20 reps - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: Full range, pause at top

---

### Saturday - Legs & Glutes
1. **Trap Bar Deadlifts** - 4 × 5-6 reps - Rest 2 min
   - Form tip: Neutral spine, equal weight distribution, drive through heels
   
2. **Belt Squat** - 3 × 8-10 reps - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Full range, no knee cave, stable torso
   
3. **Bulgarian Split Squats** - 3 × 8-10 per leg - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: Rear foot elevated, front knee tracking, control
   
4. **Leg Press Calf Raises** - 3 × 12-15 reps - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: Full range, explosive push
   
5. **Abductor Machine** - 3 × 12-15 reps - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: Smooth motion, full range

---

## Cool-down & Stretching (5-10 min)

**Static Stretches (hold 30 sec each):**
- Chest (doorway stretch)
- Hamstrings (standing or seated)
- Quadriceps (standing)
- Shoulders (cross-body)
- Lower back (knee to chest)
- Hip flexors (lunge position)

---

## Progression Strategy

### Week 1-2: Baseline
- Focus on form, controlled tempo
- Record all weights used

### Week 3-4: Volume Increase
- Add 1-2 reps to each exercise
- OR add 5 lbs to compound lifts
- Keep tempo controlled

### Week 5-6: Intensity Focus
- Reduce reps by 1-2, increase weight by 5-10 lbs
- Focus on 6-8 rep range on compounds

### Week 7-8: Deload
- Reduce volume by 30%
- Maintain form, recover

**Repeat cycle, aiming for 5-10 lb increases on major lifts every 4-6 weeks**

---

## Elbow Pain Management

⚠️ **Avoid these movements completely:**
- Barbell Curls
- Skull Crushers
- Overhead Tricep Extensions
- Heavy Isolation Curls

✅ **Elbow-friendly alternatives used in program:**
- Machine work (safer, controlled range)
- Face Pulls (rear delt, light weight)
- Lateral Raises (smooth, controlled)
- Chest Dips (if pain-free)

**If elbow pain increases during any exercise:**
- STOP immediately
- Reduce weight by 20%
- Switch to machine variation
- Consider extra rest day

For muscle gain at this intensity:
- **Protein:** 0.8-1g per lb bodyweight
- **Calories:** Slight surplus (+300-500 cal/day)
- **Carbs:** 2-3g per lb (fuel for 6-day split)
- **Hydration:** 0.5-1 oz per lb bodyweight daily
