### Monday - Push + Barbell Bench

**Skill work (6 min):**
1. **Tuck Planche Hold** - 4 × 10-12 sec - Rest 60 sec
   - Form tip: push the floor away, protract shoulders, knees tucked tight, elbows slightly bent. Hold the clean time you reached in Week 4 and add 1-2 sec per set; if 12 sec × 4 is clean, swap the last 2 sets for Advanced Tuck Lean holds.

**Volume work (~40 min):**
2. **Barbell Bench Press** - 4 × 6-8 - Rest 2 min
   - Form tip: no pause this week. Start from your Week 4 weight (52.5 + bar) and add 2.5 kg only if every set hit 8. Grip just outside shoulder width, elbows ~45° from the body, wrists stacked over elbows, bar touches mid-chest. Don't slam the lockout. Use a spotter or safety pins.
3. **Pike Push-ups** - 3 × 8-10 - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: feet on a box now that 10 clean reps are there. Head goes in front of the hands (tripod shape). Finish the last set with 3-4 wall handstand push-ups (partial range is fine) like you did in Week 4.
4. **Weighted Dips (bar)** - 3 × 8-10 - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: normal tempo, no hard lockout, slight forward lean. Keep the 20 kg disc only if the elbow stays quiet; finish with the declined push-ups if you want more volume.
5. **Kettlebell Lateral Raise** - 3 × 12-15 - Rest 45 sec
   - Form tip: you did 3 × 10 with 8 kg, so hit 12 reps at that weight before going heavier. Smooth motion, no swinging (elbow-friendly)

### Tuesday - Pull + Barbell Row

**Main work (~45 min):**
1. **Weighted Pull-ups (bar)** - 4 × 5-6 - Rest 2 min

2. **Bent-Over Barbell Rows** - 4 × 8-10 - Rest 90 sec
   - Form tip: add load only if all sets hit 10. **Overhand grip** (underhand rows load the biceps tendon at the elbow). Hinge to ~45°, flat back, pull the bar to the lower ribs, squeeze shoulder blades, lower under control. Log the exact plates this time.

## Elbow Pain Management

⚠️ **Avoid these movements completely:**
- Barbell Curls
- Skull Crushers
- Overhead Tricep Extensions
- Heavy Isolation Curls
- Close-grip bench press, underhand barbell rows, mixed-grip deadlifts

✅ **Elbow-friendly guidance for this plan:**
- Keep elbows soft/micro-bent (never locked out) during all planche holds, dips, pike and push-up variations
- Barbell bench: medium grip, elbows ~45°, no bouncing or slamming the lockout
- Back squat: loose grip, hands wide enough that the elbows aren't cranked back
- Rows and deadlifts: overhand grip only; straps are fine
- Hammer curls: light-moderate, neutral grip, no swinging. Clap push-ups: soft elbows on landing.
- No slow negatives on dips. Tempo work is limited to pulls, squats and wide push-ups.

**If elbow pain increases during any exercise:**
- STOP immediately
- Reduce weight by 20% (or shorten hold time for planche work)
- On a barbell lift: swap for the dumbbell/kettlebell version (bench → Dumbbell Incline Press, row → Kettlebell Single-Arm Row, back squat → Kettlebell Goblet Squats, deadlift → Kettlebell Deadlift)
- Drop the clap push-ups and hammer curls first
- Regress one step on the planche progression
