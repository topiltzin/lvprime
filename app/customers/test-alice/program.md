# Alice's Week 1 Program

## Goal
Build consistent workout habit

## Weekly Schedule
### Monday: Upper Body
- Push-ups: 3 sets x 10 reps
- Rows: 3 sets x 10 reps
- Rest: 60 seconds

### Wednesday: Lower Body  
- Squats: 3 sets x 12 reps
- Lunges: 3 sets x 10 reps
- Rest: 45 seconds

### Friday: Cardio
- 20 minutes jogging
- 5 minutes cool-down

## Progression (Week 2)
- Increase reps by 2 on upper body
